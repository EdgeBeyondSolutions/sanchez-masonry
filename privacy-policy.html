Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Sanchez Masonry ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through sanchezmasonry.com, how we use it, and the choices you have — in plain language, without legal filler.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you submit the "Free Estimate" form on this site, we collect the details you enter — typically your name, phone number, email address, property address or service area, and a description of your project. We do not require an account, and we do not collect payment information through this website.

Like most websites, our hosting and form infrastructure automatically logs basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and spam prevention. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your estimate request and schedule your on-site visit
  • To communicate with you about your project by phone, text, or email
  • To keep records for scheduling, invoicing, and warranty purposes once you become a customer
  • To improve our services and this website

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

A small number of trusted services help us run this website. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Netlify Forms — processes and delivers the estimate requests you submit, so our team can receive and respond to them.
  • Google Maps — embedded on our site to show our service area; loading the map may allow Google to set cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — used to display the typography on this site; your browser connects to Google's font servers to load them.

We do not use advertising trackers, social media pixels, or analytics software that profiles individual visitors.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded Google Map referenced above may set cookies managed by Google, not by us. You can control or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us, including relying on reputable, security-conscious providers (Netlify, Google) for the services above. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we do not store sensitive data such as payment or government ID information on this website.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information from our records, where we are not required to keep it (for example, for completed-job or warranty documentation)
  • Stop contacting you for marketing purposes

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults seeking home and property improvements. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Significant changes will be reflected here.
